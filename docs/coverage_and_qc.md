# Coverage and technical qualification

Keep these concepts separate: present in the assay's measured gene axis; measured but zero in this cell; detected in this cell; unmapped; absent from the measured axis; and eligible for the declared score definition. Union-matrix padding does not establish measurement. The workflow cannot independently discover a falsely declared measured-gene axis.

Original S Strict qualification uses at least three measured Seed genes and three nonzero Seeds per cell. Low-detection cells receive NA, even when a raw audit score is finite. Partial Seed scoring retains its own effective-member namespace/hash. The minimum does not certify accuracy; prior ablations and depth-selection analyses limit its biological interpretation.

Expanded S follows the frozen complete mapped-member/component policy. The existing L/A01 coverage and macrophage applicability rules remain separate. Mapping loss counts against the relevant reference definition. Sparse targeted human panels can therefore leave most Expanded programs non-evaluable.

`seed-details --run ... --cell-id ... --program-id ...` returns the exact detected, measured-zero, not-measured and unmapped lists for one cell/Original S feature. The artifact verifies its evidence hashes. `audit` mode retains raw evidence but does not upgrade a failed Strict score. Missing score-only detection evidence does not certify Original S values.

Filtering can select high-RNA cells and change the population entering a pair association. Inspect `n_target_cells`, the measured/detection counts, and actual pair-valid/eligible denominators. A valid technical score still requires a context-appropriate biological interpretation.
